FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear most.

How long does a full detail take?

Typically three to six hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

Do you come to me?

Ask about mobile availability in your area. We need access to the vehicle and, ideally, a water spigot and outlet.

What's the difference between wax and ceramic coating?

Wax lasts weeks; a coating lasts years. We'll explain what makes sense for how you use the vehicle.

What areas do you serve?

Colorado Springs and the surrounding area.

Can you remove pet hair and odors?

Yes, with the right process. Tell us about it when you book so we allow time.
