## Verdict: MARGINAL AT ASK — works at ~$155–160K, thin at $175K

The deal's profitability hinges entirely on whether the $285K ARV is real. Zip-level
data says it's optimistic:

- 28304 median sale price: **~$210–214K** (Redfin, trailing data through late 2025/2026)
- 28304 median price per sq ft: **~$146/sq ft, down 7.3% YoY** (softening market)
- 28304 median list price: **~$225K** (July 2026)
- At the zip median $/sq ft: 1,834 × $146 = **~$268K**
- The claimed $285K ARV requires **~$155/sq ft** — top-decile for the zip, only
  achievable with a fully renovated product in one of the better pockets

A conservative underwriting ARV is **$260–270K**, not $285K+.

## Rehab Estimate Reality Check

$30K for flooring throughout 1,834 sq ft + kitchen + 2 baths + fixtures + basement
paneling is **tight but plausible** for a cosmetic-grade flip:

- Flooring (LVP, ~1,800 sq ft): $7–9K
- Kitchen (cabinets painted/replaced, counters, appliances): $10–14K
- Two bath refreshes: $5–8K
- Light fixtures, hardware, punch list: $2–3K
- Basement paneling finish: $2–4K (optional — skip if budget slips)

Budget **$35–40K with contingency**. A 1965 house demands inspection of:
- **Electrical panel** — Federal Pacific / Zinsco panels common in this era ($2–3K to replace)
- **Plumbing** — cast iron drains / galvanized supply lines
- **Basement moisture** — rare for Fayetteville; verify no water intrusion before finishing it
- **Windows** — originals will hurt appraisal at a $285K price point
- Verify roof age (<10 yr) and HVAC (~5 yr) claims with receipts/permits

Also verify with Cumberland County records whether the 1,834 sq ft includes any
basement area — if heated sq ft above grade is smaller, the ARV drops further.

## Terms Flags

- **$3K EMD is high** for a wholesale assignment (typical $1–2K). Confirm it's
  refundable during a due-diligence period before wiring anything.
- Confirm you're seeing the **contract price vs. assignment fee** breakdown —
  there may be room to negotiate if the spread is fat.
- Fayetteville DOM is ~56 days; carry 6 months of holding costs in your model, not 3.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Offer $150–158K.** That's where this pencils with a realistic $265K ARV.
2. **Pull real comps before committing:** renovated 3/2 solds within 0.5 mi,
   last 90–180 days, similar sq ft. Do not underwrite off the wholesaler's ARV.
3. Walk the property (or send a GC) before EMD goes hard — price the panel,
   plumbing, windows, and basement moisture into the rehab number.
4. If the seller won't move below ~$165K, pass — at $175K you're risking ~$50K
   cash for a realistic ~$20K profit in a softening zip. That's not enough margin.
