14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be confident in your purchase. If LindaAI isn't right for you, you may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of your original purchase date.

1. Eligibility

  • Your refund request must be made within 14 days of the original purchase.
  • The guarantee applies to LindaAI product purchases, including add-ons such as the Voice Pack, within the same 14-day window.

2. How to Request a Refund

Email support@send.lindaai-brain.com from the address you used to purchase, and include your order number or purchase email. You may also request a refund directly through our Merchant of Record, Paddle, using the receipt they emailed you.

3. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are issued through Paddle (our Merchant of Record) back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take several business days for the funds to appear.

4. License Deactivation

When a refund is issued, your LindaAI license is deactivated and revoked, and access to the product ends. You agree to stop using and to delete any copies of the software.

5. Exclusions

  • Requests made after the 14-day window.
  • Cases of fraud, abuse, chargeback abuse, or violation of our Terms of Service.

6. Questions

Need help or have a question about your order? Email support@send.lindaai-brain.com — we typically reply within one business day.
