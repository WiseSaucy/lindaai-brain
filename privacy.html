Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LindaAI ("LindaAI," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by LindaAI Brain LLC, a Colorado limited liability company, collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit lindaai-brain.com or purchase and use our products. By using our website or products, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, and (optionally) business name when you purchase, start a trial, or contact support.
  • Payment information: all payments are processed by our payment provider and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. We do not see or store your full card number or banking details.
  • License & device data: a license key and a one-way device identifier ("machine ID") used to activate your product and prevent piracy.
  • Trial anti-abuse data: when you request a free trial key, we compute a one-way fingerprint hash in your browser from device characteristics (such as user agent, language, screen size, timezone, hardware profile, and canvas rendering) and store only that hash to limit repeat trial signups. The hash cannot be reversed to identify you or reveal those details.
  • Communications: emails and support messages you send us.
  • Usage data: standard, non-identifying website analytics such as pages visited and approximate region.

2. Your Work Stays On Your Machine

LindaAI runs locally on your own computer inside Claude Code. Your prompts, files, business data, and work product stay on your machine. We do not collect, transmit, read, or store the content you create or process with LindaAI.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver, activate, and support your product.
  • To process payments and refunds through Paddle.
  • To send you transactional emails (license delivery, receipts, product updates).
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and license piracy.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us operate, including Paddle (payments, billing, and tax), our email and hosting providers, and where required by law. These providers are bound to protect your data.

5. Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed for the site and secure checkout to function. Our payment provider may set cookies during checkout. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention

We keep purchase and license records for as long as needed to provide support, honor refunds, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR (EU/UK) or CCPA (California), including the right to access, delete, or opt out of the sale of personal data (we do not sell data).

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

9. Children

LindaAI is intended for business owners and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be posted on this page.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Policy or your data? Email support@send.lindaai-brain.com.
